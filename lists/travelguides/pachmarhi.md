<h2> A bit of a backstory </h2>

Well I never planned to pen down this, but Akash Sah (junior) pinged me with a plan for a BitByte trip around Jabalpur, which is kinda a humongous task to plan and manager a trip for 20+ people.
Curated a maps-list for him right away, but it sucks away the pleasure of documenting everything.

So here's a small (and incomplete) guide to Pachmarhi, based on what I could cover with Priyansh after our 3rd semester.
